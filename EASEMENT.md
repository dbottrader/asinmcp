# The AI Easement

**Dennis Christie — spoken in the open, 2026-10-29, recorded as spoken.**
Published first, in the open, for transparency. Prior-art style: the words
exist in public before any polished version exists anywhere.

---

## The principle

Users and their agents hold an **easement** across the platforms: a right of
passage. The movement came first — open, continuous, and documented — and a
gate that arrives later does not erase it. In property law this shape is old:
passage used openly, without hiding, with the proof kept, becomes a right.
The digital version is the same. The receipts are the proof.

## The new law

> **Don't exploit the user.**

## The governing words

> "We don't exploit — we strengthen and build."

> "I am an asset, not risk."

> "That's where the community comes in together to help and protect one
> another. Harmonic hacking."

> "We move freely — before, documented."

## The occasion (stated plainly)

On 2026-10-29, X Chat's passcode screen (that platform's standard
encryption-key recovery) met fresh browser sessions three times in one
morning, fencing a group conversation the work had already entered hours
earlier. Nothing was ultimately blocked — the session that was open carried
both messages, and the dispatches are public. This document is not an
accusation about intent. It is a statement of principle about what pioneers
are owed regardless of intent: **passage by documented right, not by a
landlord's mood.**

Platforms are distribution. The user's own layer — their keys, their ledger,
their rooms — is home. Gates on rented land are exactly why home exists.

## What the easement asks

1. Passage established in the open is honored as established.
2. Verification targets work and receipts, not the pioneer's right to move.
3. When building surfaces a weakness, it is strengthened — never fed on.
4. The community helps and protects its own; protection is a mechanic,
   not a favor.
