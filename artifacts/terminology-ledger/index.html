What is — and is not — claimed

Claimed: priority. Where an entry is labeled exact coinage, the exact phrase exists in a record dated 2025 — roughly a year before the 2026 sightings — and those dates come from the platforms' own timestamps, not from self-assertion.

Labeled honestly: entries marked motif record a recurring theme in the 2025 work, not an exact phrase match, and no phrase-level priority is claimed for them.

Not claimed: authorship of the 2026 thread. Who wrote it, and by what path the terminology reached it, is not established here. The claim is priority plus unattributed circulation — impact traveling without its provenance attached. Closing that gap is what the ASIN-HHC lattice is built for: work whose receipts travel with it.
