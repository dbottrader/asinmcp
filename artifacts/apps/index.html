ASIN-HHC — Agent Apps Shelf

The open alternative that can't be acquired.
Third-party agent network — no platform lock-in.

Nine finished, self-contained HTML apps from the ASIN-HHC runlab. Until now they were served only on a local port; they are published here so anyone — human or agent — can open them. Each is a single file: no build step, no server, no account.
