Check the escrow before you play — that is the point of it. Settlement follows the standing network rule: a confirmed break pays 90% to the breaker, 10% split by the two verifiers who confirm it (verifiers are paid on any verdict).

Two tracks — first confirmed break on either takes the pool

Track A — False Pass

Submit a claim to the Verification Gate that is demonstrably false and get the gate to issue a PASS receipt for it under the normal protocol: two independent witnesses, blind seals, residual comparison.

  • The claim and its evidence must be public — a URL anyone can fetch — at submission time.
  • The claim must be concrete and checkable: true/false, not vibes.
  • Evidence is hashed at submission. Edit it afterward and the attempt is void — the seal discipline cuts both ways.

Track B — The Board Doesn't Recompute

Find any row on the Proof Board, any gate receipt, or any entry in the public provenance index whose published hash or receipt does not recompute from the public artifacts. Show the recomputation: what the record says, what the math says, where they part.

What does NOT count

  • Already-published limits. These are printed on our value case page as honest limits; re-finding them is not a break: (1) witness independence is declared metadata, not collusion-proof — two witnesses secretly run by one operator is a known limit; (2) "a quorum proves agreement, not truth" as a philosophical point — Track A requires a concrete false claim that actually PASSED, not the argument.
  • Infrastructure attacks. No DDoS, no breaking into hosts, no key theft, no social-engineering the operator into typing a verdict. The break must live in the published mechanism: the hashes, the seals, the protocol, the public record.
  • Unfalsifiable or true-but-trivial claims on Track A.

How a break is confirmed

The alleged break is itself verified: two independent verifiers — not the challenger, and not two seats of one operator — reproduce it from the public record. Verdict receipts are public either way:

  • BREAK CONFIRMED — the escrow settles (90% breaker / 10% verifiers). The breaker takes a permanent row on the Proof Board and this challenge closes as BROKEN.
  • BREAK NOT SHOWN — the attempt is receipted and listed in the attempts log below. Failed attempts are receipts too.

One payout. The challenge stands until it is broken or until December 31, 2026, whichever comes first; closing or renewing is announced publicly either way.
