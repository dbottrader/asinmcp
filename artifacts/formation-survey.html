Survey verdict

The best immediately reusable records are Crabwood’s 1,368-bit HTML transcription and the Roundway Hill 2023 construction/vector materials. Neither is a coordinate survey of the original formation.

Closest published neighbors

Method note

“Opened” is not the same as “indexed.”

The research pass directly opened a subset of the strongest pages. Search-index leads are retained as leads, not confirmations. Public availability also does not imply reuse permission: most formation photography is view-only and copyrighted.
