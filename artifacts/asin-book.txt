ASIN Protocol — The Four Primitives
ASIN is a compact language: - Anchor — where and when, the situational context. - Shape —
the geometry present and its relations. - Intention — the proposed meaning tested against
evidence. ✶ Number — measures, ratios, frequencies, and the binary date stamp.

Operational Rule
Measure first, myth later. Geometry and logs precede interpretation. Intention must be falsifiable.

Binary Date Stamp (N)
We encode YYYY-MM-DD using Y11-M4-D5 bits (the project’s internal convention). Example
for 2025-09-16 → 11111101001.1001.10000. This stamp appears in filenames and indexes to
anchor provenance.

Room Rule
No porch drops. Every artifact must be walked Vault → Resonance → Workshop → Bridge →
Expansion → Archive. Skipping rooms wastes energy and breaks auditability.

Decode Protocol — Measure, Then Meaning
We treat symbolic formations (e.g., crop circles) as data. A reproducible seven-step decode is
used:
1. Image Acquisition — top-down, full-pattern, high-resolution imagery with metadata.
2. Canvas Prep — orient north up, mark center and scale, record coordinates.
3. Geometry Extraction — trace circles/lines, count elements, compute ratios.
4. Number & Frequency — convert ratios to simple fractions; map to harmonic bands (e.g., 432
Hz family).
5. Symbolic Candidates — propose meanings that fit the geometry without overreach.
6. Cross-Reference — compare with known formations and prior ASIN entries.
7. Report — publish a succinct decode with figures, logs, and binary stamp.

Standing Check
Every decode must pass falsifiability: another analyst with the same inputs should reach the same
geometry and measurements, even if their symbolism differs. If geometry disagrees, redo Step 3.

Proof-of-Process (P.O.P.) — Evidence Chain
P.O.P. is our audit spine. Each artifact is stamped, logged, and stored with a minimal manifest.
This protects originality claims and enables external review.
