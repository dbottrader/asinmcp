CP8 is Dennis M. Christie’s human–AI provenance and evidence-governance framework (ASIN-HHC) applied to the geometric morphology of aerial / crop formations. Its core discipline: every claim must trace to an executed run, an input, and a SHA-256 receipt before it can move from OBSERVED → TESTED → PROMOTED. PROMOTION: HOLD

Section · External check · Verified 2026-10-06

OpenAI convergence: conceptual only, no connection

CP8 was cross-checked against OpenAI’s openai/math corpus. The result is a clean negative on connection — and a genuine, narrower convergence of method.

No direct connection — 0 hits, both directions

GitHub code search found no CP8, ASIN-HHC, Holbrook or Roundway in openai/math, and no Kakeya, Mahler, Riemann, Navier or Hodge in Dennis’s repos. Corpus scale for reference: 722 manuscripts, 372 families, ~4,000 problems, with Lean formalizations. Any claim of derivation or direct link is unsupported. Two precision notes: the corpus’s Riemann result is quasi-Riemann (zero-free region Re s > 7/8) — not a Riemann Hypothesis solution; its Navier–Stokes claim is separate and not Clay-accepted.

Where they genuinely converge

Method, not subject matter

  • Checkable artifacts: Lean certificates + source SHA-256 and run receipts on their side; hash / receipt / replay / promotion gates on CP8’s. Same instinct, different guarantee — Lean proves correctness; a hash proves byte integrity only.
  • Harmonic / spectral tools used on geometric structure in both programmes.
  • Planar geometry primitives (circles, graphs) — pointed at different questions.

Per the 2026-07-30 provenance record: similarity is conceptual convergence unless direct derivation is independently demonstrated. It has not been.
