01

Evidence posture

The case is rich in reported measurements but poor in accessible records.

The object may be interesting. Its extraordinary properties are not established.

The public case rests on examinations within Jaime Maussan’s circle, media summaries, and witness testimony. The reported alloy is terrestrial; the fiber, dating, mass, thermal, and signal claims lack public raw data and independent replication. Mainstream assessments favor a manufactured terrestrial artifact.

05

Did it “grow”?

The reported change is mass, not physical growth; even the direction varies by source.

Recovery
2 kg, attributed to finder José Arias Restrepo.
~2 months
About 10 kg, claimed by David Vélez after private storage at Germany Company offices.
Variants
Other reports cite an 8.1 kg baseline, 2→16 kg over five months, or a drop from ~10→2 kg in a speculative model.
No independent weigh log exists. There is no continuous custody record, calibrated scale series, time-stamped protocol, environmental control, or outside witness set for the alleged 2→10 kg increase. The available record does not establish mass change.
06

Chain of claims

Confidence falls where custody and documentation break.

02 MAR 2025

Reported recovery

A silent sphere was said to zigzag, change altitude abruptly, strike or brush a high-voltage line, and land near Alto Bonito outside Buga.

MAR–APR 2025

Private custody

Handled by Arias and David Vélez; stored at a metal-detector business. This period is central to the chain-of-custody critique.

MAY 2025

X-ray accounts

Velázquez reported three layers, nine microspheres, a central core, and no welds. Later counts changed.

30 MAY 2025

Fiber claim

Press material attributed 52 internal fibers to specialists linked with UNAM. No institutional report followed.

09 JUL 2025

Acoustic test

At IPN, the team reported vibration at 190–300 Hz, a temperature rise, and EMF response.

12 SEP 2025 → 2026

Resin & second sphere

A ~6 mg resin sample was reportedly received for dating. In 2026, a separate 13 kg sphere with 29 black dots entered the story.

08

What would settle it?

Extraordinary language is unnecessary. Good metrology is decisive.

Independent materials panel

Sample under documented custody. Publish calibrated elemental spectra, micrographs, isotope data, blank controls, and uncertainty. Repeat at two unaffiliated laboratories.

Answers: composition & isotope claims

Raw volumetric imaging

Release DICOM CT data, X-ray projections, scan settings, phantoms, segmentation rules, and an outside radiology read. Counts should not shift by narration.

Answers: layers, fibers, core, microspheres, torus

Controlled signal chamber

Use shielded baselines, dummy aluminum spheres, calibrated antennas, accelerometers, microphones, and thermal probes. Pre-register frequencies and analysis before stimulation.

Answers: emissions, resonance & thermal response

Custodied mass series

Two calibrated balances, tamper-evident enclosure, environmental logging, continuous video, and independent observers. Report raw time series—not selected endpoints.

Answers: “growth” / weight change

Resin authentication

Document sampling location and custody, chemistry, contamination controls, and replicate dates. Keep the inference narrow: resin age is not object age.

Answers: organic sample age only

Manufacturing comparison

Compare seams, alloy, forming marks, interior components, and dimensions against industrial tanks, art fabrication, optical fiber, and known joining methods.

Answers: terrestrial fabrication hypotheses
