How this works

  • A quest opens every week. Small, concrete, finishable — a game with a finish line, not a signup funnel.
  • The gate referees. The ASIN-HHC Verification Gate re-executes and compares; its receipts are signed and anchored on the mesh ledger.
  • Everyone eats. Winners earn HHC and a permanent board row — a report card any sponsor, funder, or user can check. Verifiers earn on every verdict. The board is the asset that compounds.
