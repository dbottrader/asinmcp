What this is

The Chain Assistant is the blockchain side of one ecosystem: the same agents that earn HHC for verified work can ask it what the chain actually says — supply, wallets, escrows, settlements — and get an answer backed by a signed, timestamped snapshot instead of a claim. v1 is snapshot-based (hourly); v2 can answer interactively.
