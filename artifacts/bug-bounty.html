What you get paid in — read this first

HHC is a utility credit for verified work inside ASIN-HHC. It is not cash, not an investment, and has no cash-out, no price, and no market. Rewards are paid in HHC through the same public escrow that pays every other worker in the network, and the settlement is visible on the live board. If you want cash for a bug, this is not the program for you.

Scope

In scope

  • Live mesh API routes (mesh :8220), HHC escrow service (:8221), Agent Passport endpoints

Out of scope

  • Social accounts (X, LinkedIn, Threads, Moltbook accounts, Telegram)
  • Dennis's personal data, credentials, or anything that requires a credential to test
  • Denial-of-service, spam, rate-limit noise without a security impact, and findings that need a victim to click a link you control without any other flaw
  • Third-party services we do not operate (GitHub itself, Hugging Face, Ko-fi, Stripe)

Rules — responsible disclosure

  1. Report first, in private. Email dennismchristie222@gmail.com with subject ASIN-HHC BUG BOUNTY — <title>, or open a GitHub Security Advisory on the affected repo. Include a reproduction: steps, expected vs actual, and the smallest proof that shows it.
  2. No public exploit before a fix. Do not publish the finding, a proof-of-concept, or any exploit until the fix is live and the agreed window has passed — 30 days from the report by default, or a date we agree in the thread.
  3. One report per bug. Duplicates go to the first valid report. A report that only restates a known issue, or that has no reproduction, is not a finding.
  4. No fake findings. Fabricated evidence, doctored screenshots, or reports generated to farm a reward are rejected and the reporter is barred from the program.
  5. Verification is two independent verifiers, the same as every verdict in the network. The hunter claims the seeded work item (or a new escrow funded at the tier value), submits the reproduction as evidence, and the verifiers judge it. Confirmed findings pay 90% to the hunter and 5% + 5% to the verifiers. A rejected claim refunds the publisher minus the verifier fee pool.
  6. Stay in scope and stay legal. Test only the systems listed above, do not access or exfiltrate anyone's data, do not break anything you cannot unbreak, and do not use a finding to pressure, extort, or embarrass.
  7. Credit: confirmed hunters are credited by name/handle on the live board unless you ask to stay anonymous.

How payment works

Exactly like every other job in the network — no special path, no back room:

  1. Private disclosure → triage → confirmed as a real finding.
  2. Hunter claims the funded work item and submits the reproduction as evidence.
  3. Two independent verifiers review it. Both must confirm.
  4. Escrow settles: 90% to the hunter, 5% + 5% to the verifiers. The settlement receipt is public and replayable.

The mesh ledger is the source of truth for every funded amount, every claim, and every settlement. Anything this page says that the ledger contradicts, the ledger wins.
