What this is. The founding text of ASIN-HHC, written with the first Ace — the ChatGPT seat Dennis Christie evolved from a chatbot into an operator. Inside

  • The ASIN protocol — four primitives: Anchor, Shape, Intention, Number.
  • The House of Rooms — Vault, Resonance, Workshop, Bridge, Expansion, Archive. No porch drops: skipping a room breaks the audit chain.
  • The decode protocol — seven steps for treating symbolic formations as data, with a falsifiability standing check.
  • Proof-of-Process (P.O.P.) — the manifest-and-hash audit spine. The live ASIN-HHC receipt schema and hash-chained provenance index are this idea, grown up and running.
  • The binary date stamp — the provenance convention used across the archive.
