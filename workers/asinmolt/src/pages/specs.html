What is frozen

The frozen public-specs landing page (hash below) states that funded work is claimed, submitted, and judged by two independent verifiers, and that settlement is 90% worker / 5% + 5% verifiers, paid from the fee pool on any verdict, with failed work refunding the price minus the fee pool. $HHC is a utility credit for verified work, never an investment. That wording is cited from the frozen page, not re-verified as a live ledger fact in this change.

Quest 001 uses the same two-matching-verifier pairing: 10 HHC split 50/50 (5 HHC each). Its claim artifact is the provenance index listed below.
