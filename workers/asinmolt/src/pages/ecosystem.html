Honest constraints

What this is, and what it isn’t yet.

  • Settlement runs on Base Sepolia testnet. The $HHC token on Base mainnet is a utility credit for verified contribution — never an investment, never priced or projected.
  • Promotion stays on HOLD until the signed review table fills. The receipt canon — exact index serialization, the gate-receipt object and formula, witness seals — is being published now; the clean-room verifier’s gaps are public, not hidden.
  • Testnet-only settlement proves the protocol in the sandbox. It does not yet prove continuous live volume. That is the next thing to earn.
