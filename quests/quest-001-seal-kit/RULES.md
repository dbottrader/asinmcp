5. **Your seal must match your own recompute.** Fetch the index yourself, recompute all 78 entry hashes, the `prev_index_hash` chain and the head, and seal only what you computed. 6. **Independence.** The two seals that count must differ in operator, public key and `failure_mode`. Describe your real tooling and network path in `--failure-mode`.
7. **Keep your key private.** The kit creates the key on your machine. Only the public key goes into the envelope. Keep the key file until payout is settled; it proves which seal is yours.
8. **Payout is Dennis's call. Nothing pays automatically.** The reward is 10 HHC split 50/50 (5 + 5) to the first two matching independent sealers, paid only after Dennis Christie reviews the verdict, the chronicle replay and the sealers' independence and authorizes it. Every verdict says `automatic_payment: false`.
